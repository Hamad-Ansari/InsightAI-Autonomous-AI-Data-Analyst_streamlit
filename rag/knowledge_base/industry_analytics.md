# Industry-Specific Analytical Guidance

InsightAI is domain-agnostic. Common use cases by industry:

## E-commerce / Retail
- AOV, revenue per category, conversion, cart abandonment, repeat purchase rate.

## Banking / Finance
- Transaction volumes, fraud/transaction anomalies, customer segments, risk.

## Marketing / Sales
- Campaign ROI, lead conversion, channel performance, funnel drop-off.

## HR
- Headcount, attrition, time-to-hire, diversity, pay bands.

## Healthcare
- Patient volumes, readmission, resource utilisation, appointment trends.

## Manufacturing / Supply Chain
- Output, defect rate, downtime, lead time, inventory.

## SaaS / Operations
- MRR, churn, feature adoption, support tickets, uptime.

## Customer Support
- Ticket volume, sentiment, resolution time, CSAT.

The same four dimensions (composition, distribution, comparison, relationship)
apply universally; only the meaning of the KPIs differs.
