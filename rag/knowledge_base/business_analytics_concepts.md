# Business Analytics Concepts

Business analytics turns data into decisions. Common metrics and dimensions:

## Metrics (KPIs)
- Revenue, sales, profit, cost, margin, growth rate, churn, retention, ARPU,
  conversion rate, customer lifetime value (CLV).
- For HR: headcount, attrition, time-to-hire.
- For logistics: on-time delivery, lead time, cost per shipment.

## Dimensions
- Region, segment, product, category, channel, customer type, date, campaign.

## Analysis types
- **Descriptive** — what happened?
- **Diagnostic** — why did it happen?
- **Predictive** — what is likely to happen?
- **Prescriptive** — what should we do about it?

## Best practices
- Define KPIs clearly and tie them to business objectives.
- Compare against benchmarks (targets, prior period, industry).
- Separate facts from interpretation; validate before acting.
