# Sentiment Analysis Concepts

Sentiment analysis classifies text (reviews, comments, feedback) as positive,
neutral or negative.

## Approaches
- **Lexicon-based (e.g. VADER)** — dictionary of words with valence scores;
  fast, free, works well on short social-style text.
- **Pre-trained transformer models** — higher accuracy, heavier resources.
- **Multilingual models** — for non-English text.

## Outputs
- Percentage of positive / neutral / negative texts.
- Average compound sentiment score (range about -1 to +1).
- Frequently used positive and negative words.
- Common themes and topics.

## Applications
- Customer feedback, product reviews, support tickets, social media mentions.
- Track sentiment **over time** to detect shifts.
- Compare sentiment **by category / product / region** to prioritise action.
