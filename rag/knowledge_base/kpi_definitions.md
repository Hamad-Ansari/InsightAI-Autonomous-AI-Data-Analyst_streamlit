# KPI Definitions

- **Revenue** — total income from sales before costs.
- **Sales** — total units or monetary amount sold.
- **Profit** — revenue minus cost.
- **Margin** — profit expressed as a percentage of revenue.
- **Conversion rate** — percentage of visitors/leads that convert to a desired
  action (e.g., purchase).
- **Churn / attrition rate** — percentage of customers who stop using a service
  within a period.
- **Retention rate** — percentage of customers who remain.
- **Growth rate** — percentage change in a metric over a period.
- **Average order value (AOV)** — total revenue divided by number of orders.
- **Customer lifetime value (CLV)** — expected revenue from a customer over time.
- **ARPU** — average revenue per user.
- **On-time delivery** — percentage of shipments delivered on schedule.
- **NPS** — Net Promoter Score; a measure of customer loyalty.
